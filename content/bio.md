I am currently an M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at the State Key Laboratory of Digital Manufacturing Equipment and Technology, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), advised by Prof. Chaoyong Zhang. I obtained my B.S. degree in Industrial Engineering from HUST in 2024.

My research focuses on contrastive learning, unsupervised domain adaptation, and physics-guided deep learning for analyzing and fusing multi-sensor time-series signals to enable tool wear prediction across varying operating conditions, with an emphasis on improving model robustness and generalization. I also have extensive research experience in semantic segmentation and unsupervised domain adaptation, developing models based on YOLO and U-Net for cross-domain tool wear recognition, particularly for transferring knowledge from high-quality laboratory images to low-quality images collected in real-world industrial environments.

As a core developer of a national key research project, I have led the development of multiple digital twin systems based on WebGL and Unity3D, gaining extensive experience in 3D scene construction, cyber–physical interaction, and real-time data synchronization.
